Questions before getting started?

Here are a few things to help you decide whether LocalBoost is the right fit.

Who do you work with?

We support small businesses across Canada, including contractors, home-service providers, shops and independent professionals.

What do I receive each month?

Your selected package includes the stated number of social media posts and the creative support listed above. We confirm the details and delivery plan before starting.

Do I need to provide photos and a logo?

Share your logo, photos, brand colours and business information if you have them. We’ll discuss what is available and what is needed during setup.

Paid advertising budgets are separate. The monthly packages shown here cover the listed content and creative services, not ad spend.

How do I get started?

Choose a package or email us first. We’ll confirm the scope, platforms, schedule and next steps before beginning your monthly service.

Where are you located?

LocalBoost Digital is based in Fredericton, New Brunswick, and serves small businesses throughout Canada.
